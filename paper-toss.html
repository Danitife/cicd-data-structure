What happens when you click Toss?

  1. Make a ball. A class is a recipe. new PaperBall(position) follows that recipe to make one ball. The constructor creates its circle on the page. this means that particular ball.
  2. Send it flying. ball.fly() asks that ball to move. The browser animates its top position from 83% to 23%. Smaller numbers are higher on the screen, so it moves up.
  3. Check where it landed. When the animation ends, check whether the ball is lined up with the bin, report the result, and remove the ball. The next click creates a new one.
